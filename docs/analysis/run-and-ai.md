# Midgard Survivors 0.2.3 — run and autopilot

Source study of the effective `analysis/src/` overlay (base + bridge + 0.2.3 patch). References are `file.gd:line` in that tree. Source observations are distinguished from deductions and farming judgments (`[INFERENCE]`). This is not a runtime benchmark or a bot design.

## 1. Player build and level-up draft

- `Player.setup(class_id, profile)` starts from `GameData.CLASSES[class_id]` (custom Super Novice uses `sn_def`), with first ammo, pet and appearance from the profile; raw STR/AGI/VIT/INT/DEX/LUK are class defaults + rebirth points on every stat + permanent class-specific stats. This sum becomes `base_stats`, separate from upgrades obtained in the run. It learns the starter, loads permanent equipment, album cards, pacts, dishes, meta modifiers and extra lives; applies prep bans/favorites; recomputes and fills HP. `profile_of` reads the corresponding Save settings, loadout and card album. `player.gd:521–635,640–670`
- `GameData.SKILLS` is the per-ID table, not the hero's equipped set: records specify `type`, base damage/cooldown/range/area or heal/shield amounts, `per_level`, and optional traits such as element, status, prerequisite or evolution. `Skills.cast` interprets the type, queries `skill_value`, and handles per-skill targeting and modifiers. For scale: `bash` is a 220° melee arc, base damage `30`, cooldown `0.75`, range `3.5`; per level `+10` damage, `−0.07` cooldown, `+0.25` range, `+0.04` stun probability. `fire_bolt` starts at `23` damage, two bolts, range `10`, cooldown `1`. `game_data.gd:2701–2735,4431–4435,11664–11683`; `skills.gd:57–117`
- `skill_pool()` = base class skills + current job skills + eligible copied non-passives; `passive_pool()` does the equivalent for passives. New choices require their `needs` skill or its evolution and available category slots. Defaults: five non-passives and two passives; second job adds one each, reborn second job adds one more passive, and class/job/Sage overrides can change these counts. Passives don't get an active cast timer. Job change at Lv20 replaces the class look/job weapon as applicable, adds the job starter, recomputes, and refills HP. `player.gd:825–879,978–985,1016–1019,1034–1070,1141–1157,1188–1198`; `game_data.gd:4671–4677,4691–4692,4707–4710`

**Derived values.** Let `E(stat)` be effective stat and `m[k]` the recomputed modifiers (meta, equipment/sets/cards, powers, pacts, altars, etc.). These are core formulas, not the last word for individual jobs, procs, weapons or enemies.

| Quantity | Implementation |
| --- | --- |
| Effective stat | `cut(x) = min(x,C) + 0.5*max(0,x-C)`; `E(k) = (cut(base[k]) + cut(max(0,stats[k]−base[k]) + buff_stat(k))) * (1+stat_pct[k])`. Base and run contributions hit the soft cap **separately**; `buff_stat` applies aura to STR/INT. `C = MAX_STAT(10) + wing_stat_cap`; stat draft hard cap is raw `999`. `player.gd:1232–1280,1306–1314,5064–5067`; `game_data.gd:4710,4899–4901` |
| HP, regen, speed | `HP = (def.hp + 50*E(VIT) + m.max_hp)*(1 + m.max_hp_pct + .03*E(VIT))*(1+m.grade_hp)`, then feather and oath-curse modifiers; increase adds the max-HP delta to current HP; mirror `host_max_hp` may override. Regen begins at `.15 + .25*E(VIT) + def.regen + m.regen`; movement `min(class/mount cap,def.speed*(1+.03*E(AGI)+m.move))`. VIT also adds heal received and cuts non-piercing damage (up to 25% VIT cut, separate from armor). `player.gd:1512–1545,1616–1627,1942–1943,3556–3559`; `game_data.gd:4855–4865,5013` |
| Damage | `dmg_mult = (1+m.dmg+COIN_DMG*coins)*max(.1,1+m.risk_dmg)*(1+m.grade_mult)*job.dmg_more`, then Hel marks and applicable job buffs. Basic base ATK `(def.basic.atk + .72*(1+6*E(STR))) * (1+aura_blade)*_tongue_now*weapon_feel.dmg`; Mace Mastery can use higher STR/INT. Basic multiplier starts `1+.07*basic_stat()+m.basic_dmg`, plus spheres and Solitude. Skill multiplier is `(1+area_over*overflow_rate + .15*Σ(weight*E(stat))) * skill_amp`, with `skill_amp=max(.1,1+m.skill_dmg*(.5 if positive else 1))`; HP scaling substitutes `(max_hp+shield)^.43/2.6`, and some/copied skills use higher STR/INT. The hit pipeline combines base × global × basic/skill/proc × element × variance × critical modifiers, followed by further contextual factors; `player.skill_mult` is a summary, not every skill's multiplier. `player.gd:1599–1611,1635–1660,1850–1899,2430–2431,2488–2509`; `main.gd:5753–5799`; `game_data.gd:4667–4668,4717–4726,4761–4762,11480–11487` |
| ASPD and cadence | `ASPD=200−(200−base)*keep((1+6*raw_AGI)/350)*keep(max(0,aspd_bonus))`, `base=def.basic.aspd+weapon_feel.aspd+job bonus`; `keep(x)=1−x` through `.8`, then `.2/(1+5*(x−.8))`. **Raw** AGI here, not `E(AGI)`. Basic delay `max(.08,(200−ASPD)*.02)`; excessive speed yields extra hits (`.08/raw_delay`, at least 1; soft curve above 20, power `.7`, fractional extra hit rolls). Excess over `MAX_SWING_HITS` is converted to per-hit power. Skill delay uses base cooldown × `1/(1+soft_haste(.035*E(DEX)+m.haste + .02*E(AGI)*(1−INT weight)))/(1+m.grade_haste)`, with `soft_haste(h)=h/(1+max(0,h)/4)`. `player.gd:1514–1518,1658,1888–1889,2424–2478,2630–2637`; `game_data.gd:4651–4668,4745–4757,4871` |
| Crit | Raw chance `.03+.03*E(LUK)+m.crit+COIN_CRIT*coins+Solitude+applicable Fury`; actual `crit=min(raw,1)`. Multiplier `1.5+.025*E(LUK)+m.crit_dmg+applicable Fury+max(0,raw−1)`. At `crit_dmg>=2.5`, a crit is Ultra (`×1.25`); at `>=5.0` eligible crits can roll Lethal (25%, `×1.5` plus splash). Hit logic may add `crit_bonus` or force crit for sleeping targets. `player.gd:1651–1657`; `main.gd:5753–5795`; `game_data.gd:4866–4894` |

**Draft mechanics.** XP increments `level` and `pending_levels`. `World.level_choices()` returns `net.offered` to clients and cached `player.offered_choices()` to authority; `World.choose` applies and clears the offer, decrements pending levels, and chains or closes the menu. `_process` puts job change ahead of drafts; `hold_levels` can accumulate them. A normal `show_level_up` holds the world (pauses solo; party continues with local hero choosing). `player.gd:4981–4990,5011–5034`; `main.gd:2208–2215,4489–4504,14933–15003`; `menus.gd:5543–5556`

- `run_bans[class_id]` are prep bans limited by available Fly Wing banishes; setup consumes one each and can substitute a banned starter. Keys are `kind:id`. `run_favs` is up to two IDs, doubles eligible skill/evolution weight; it doesn't force a selection. Base weights: new skill `1.3`, new passive `1.0`, sub-Lv5 upgrade `1.5`, evolution `6`, scalable overlevel `.7`, stat `.8` before its run soft cap and `.55` thereafter. Draw without replacement (`3 + capped extras`), then try to ensure a new active (prefer base class), a stat, and at Lv≤6 an eligible `early` passive; empty pool gets a 40%-max-HP potion. Each skill/stat roll independently gets rarity weights `70/22/7/1`, shifted out of common by LUK, and advances `1/2/3/3` ranks (legendary additionally `+1` all stats). `player.gd:609–633,668–670,5020–5179,5199–5223,5265–5295`; `game_data.gd:4451–4452,4677,5896–5897,8585–8591`
- At base Lv≥5 plus its required passive, an entry in `EVOLUTIONS` unlocks a separate evolution pick (e.g. `tornado_kick + kihop → tornado_kick_x`); it replaces the base, leaves the passive, and transfers only excess levels: `EX level = 1 + max(0,base_level−5)`, regardless of the offer's display `level:10`. Scalable skills can grow beyond their effective breakpoint; `skill_cap` is 5 for base, 1 for EX. For listed fields the default overlevel factor is `1+.1*weighted_overlevels`: first five excess ranks full, next through excess 145 half, rest quarter. Per-skill overrides and excluded fields apply; e.g. `count`/`status_chance` do not generally scale further, cooldown per-level growth stops at the breakpoint unless overridden. `player.gd:5050–5063,5238–5262`; `game_data.gd:4439–4452,4513–4528,11664–11714`
- **Two automatic pickers.** Saved `endless_auto_pick` (“Elegir al azar”, despite name, not gated by Endless) / training auto uses `AutoPick.choose(choices,player.pick_order(),player.pick_limits())`. Both settings are keyed by class. For `skill`/`stat`, a positive limit rejects a choice whose resulting level exceeds it, unless every choice is filtered out; evolutions are exempt. Among ordered IDs, highest rarity wins and order breaks ties; with no matching ID, pick randomly among highest rarity. Evolutions match the base ID. In contrast, `menus.auto_pick=true` and NPC party drafts use `Bot.pick_choice`, not these preference settings (prep bans still affect the pool). `main.gd:4241–4251,4492–4504,15008–15028,15050–15059`; `menus.gd:5543–5547,11763–11796`; `player.gd:3099–3107`; `auto_pick.gd:15–43`
- **`Bot._score`.** Evolution `1000`, potion `1`; other scores multiplied by `1+.25*(steps−1)`; first tie wins. Passive `25` or `75` if it unlocks an owned evolution base. Heal/buff/shield `35`, heal `55` below 60% HP. Other skills `55`, `+15` for registered AoE/ground/orbit/summon/falcon/bolt classes, `+10` if evolvable, `+8` if owned; skill choice ending above Lv5 gets `×.6`. Stat `40` for main, `28` AGI, VIT `34` below 50% HP else `22`, others `18`; halve at current run soft cap. Main stat starts from greatest class stat, overridden by largest sum of owned damaging skills' scaling weights (HP maps to VIT, DEX to STR). No enemy, loot, chosen job plan, future hazard, or whole-build simulation in these scores. `bot.gd:19–107`

## 2. Autopilot: movement, combat and limits

`Player.input` defaults to `HeroInput.Local`. With `World.autopilot=false`, keyboard/gamepad movement (world-transformed) or mouse-walk supplies the vector; `true` **replaces** it with `world.autopilot_input()`. `HeroInput.BotHero` is an independent party input provider, not what toggling `autopilot` installs on the local hero. It gives `Bot.steer` a goal in order: nearest fallen ally → per-hero raid goal during `raid.state == "fight"` → follow non-downed lead when farther than 12 → no goal. It has no explicit NPC altar, portal-entry/exit, tower stairs, quest or Morroc goal of its own. `hero_input.gd:20–31,55–67`; `player.gd:21`; `main.gd:2114–2135,4255–4260`

- `Bot.steer` scores 16 unit directions; walkability at `.8` and `2.2` ahead, three probes `2.5` farther for room, inverse-square crowd risk from enemies in `9` (boss weight `3`, elite `1.6`), enemy-shot forecasts `.25/.5` s, attraction/repulsion from nearest enemy within `14`, previous heading and a small bias toward origin. Melee above 50% HP discounts crowd danger to `.3` and moves toward smaller packs; above 75% also approaches larger packs weakly. Ranged/casters push away inside `4`, approach beyond `7`. Goals are a heading bonus, weaker at ≤40% HP and zeroed within `.8`; there is no stationary candidate unless every direction fails. `bot.gd:8–10,136–229`
- **Loot is not ignored:** `Bot._loot_dir` weighs chest/tame/branch/card distance `×.3`, other pickups `×1`; selects the nearest weighted within `<12`, then adds only a `.6` heading term when fewer than six local enemies. That is not a collection guarantee, a rarity target, or a routing plan. **Runner is not a goal:** it is only a normal nearest-enemy/area target; the steering logic does not give it chase priority. **Generic ground AoE is not in the scores:** only nearby enemies, projectiles and terrain are evaluated. Raid/Morroc exceptions below are explicit goals, not global avoidance. `bot.gd:136–212,233–242`; `main.gd:20719–20745,15451–15465`
- **Local `world.autopilot` priority:** fallen ally (soft steering), raid goal, Morroc goal, Tower stairs, eligible altar (soft steering), quest goal (soft steering), ordinary steering. Finite raid/Morroc/stairs goals get a straight normalized vector and stop inside `.3`; no `Bot.steer` crowd/shot/walkability scoring that frame. `showcase` alone adds direction snapping, hold and unsticking. Supported raid goals include entrance/exit, pillar LoS, shield/pads, slam/rain, cone/charge, gaps, puddles, holy/bubble and object-specific mechanics; `epic.goal` and trial logic add others. Morroc has dedicated cues for impact, seals, orbit, judgement, spokes, dash, adds and pools. Party bots get raid fight goals only as soft goals; some raid `_nearest` choices are measured from `world.player`, even for an NPC. `main.gd:20676–20745`; `mvp_raid.gd:2364–2423,3582–3643,3931–3936`; `morroc_event.gd:1451–1501`
- **Attacks are already automatic independently of movement AI.** `Player.tick` takes the input vector, applies map slide/dash, and unless choosing calls `_basic_attack`. It attacks the nearest enemy in weapon/class reach (melee can fall back to a larger boss reach); on no target it rechecks in `.1` s. Normal skills attempt `Skills.cast` at cooldown; passive, stance, disabled, held and still-only skills aren't cast by that loop, and failed casts retry in `.15` s normally. Per-skill targeting is separate (e.g. heals pick neediest eligible ally; some attacks require a nearby enemy). There is no general attack-target command on `HeroInput`. `player.gd:1704–1751,1775–1816,2601–2627`; `skills.gd:57–117,490–525,1285`; `main.gd:5559–5561`
- The world flag also triggers periodic quiver selection by summed local elemental effectiveness and automatic eligible refinement, auto-generates some Tower interactions, randomizes corrupt chest alignment, suppresses Hypnotist and normal Morroc scheduling, and skips usual leaderboard/title/end-screen automatic Tower-snapshot paths. It is **not** `menus.auto_pick`. `main.gd:4505–4510,15726–15749,20749–20762`; `tower_run.gd:225–244`; `karma.gd:200–207`; `altars.gd:608–612`; `morroc_event.gd:140–149,208–228,239–249`; `menus.gd:13578–13593`

## 3. Run director and main clear condition

- `start_run` resets encounter, timing, loot and counter state, spawns/setup heroes, sets `running=true`, unpauses, and closes menus. `_process` advances map time and runs `_run_director` unless the raid/Morroc freezes time. `_run_director` exits for training, Tower or `no_spawn`; otherwise does normal spawns, ferocious enemies, oaths, events, runner, storm and Hell hazards. `_director_spawns` iterates the active `stage.spawns` entries (`from<=time<=to`), starting timers from `.5`, subtracting `delta*rate`, then adding the row's `every` and spawning `batch` at expiry (once per row per tick; swarm/warband special cases). It is also separately callable during part of Morroc, and returns if `hell_late.calm()`. `main.gd:1340–1474,4333–4352,4941–5045`
- `spawn_rate(t)=1+t/170`. With `d= danger`, `h=max(0,d−15)`, `r=clamp(t/300,0,1)`, multiply by `(1+.15*(min(d,15)−1))*(1+.15*h*r)`, `(1+altars.mod("spawn"))`, `(1+player.curse)`, opening easing, party spawn factor and (if Endless) `1+.1*_endless_count`. A present `boss.def.mvp` applies `×.4`, and active Hell tide `×3`. Some `STAGES` rows store `rate_mult`, but it isn't a factor in this implementation of `_spawn_rate_now`. Ferocious enemies have their own randomized intervals, affected by hard/Endless, altar `elite`, dark karma, and oaths. `main.gd:4921–4952,4988–4998`; `game_data.gd:9656–9679,9759,9808–9812,10165–10166,10227–10241,10271–10272,11350–11351`
- Prontera `SPAWNS`: Poring from `0` every `.9` ×1; Lunatic from `30` every `2` ×1; Fabre from `75` every `2.4` ×2; Willow from `150` every `4` ×1, all nominally through `9999`. `EVENTS` has ring waves and elites plus Angeling at `240`, Baphomet at `480`. `ring` places units 15 away around the hero; `elite` spawns offscreen (extra copies on hard), optionally sets `World.boss`; a supported map MVP opens a raid portal if raid idle, otherwise boss events spawn directly and set `World.boss`. Director reads the event list sequentially, not by scanning all eligible rows each frame. [INFERENCE] In the declared Prontera order, Mastering's `at=100` entry is blocked by the `135` ring, and rings declared after the `430` elite become due in the same tick as that elite, absent a change to the event array. `game_data.gd:9188–9208,9656–9665`; `main.gd:5033–5045,5275–5300`
- `mvp_at()` is `stage.mvp_time−altars.mod("mvp_early")` or debug meta `mvp_at`; `_event_at` subtracts the altar only for the stage MVP. With the debug override, the scheduled MVP event returns `INF` and `_process` separately opens the portal once when due/idle. Normal raid flow `idle → portal → loading → intro → fight → won`; any standing hero within `1.2` enters an unsealed portal. Opening dismisses ordinary enemies and pulls some drops; **entry/elapsed time is not a win**. The active tracked MVP death schedules the loot drain and clear; raid win grants Ymir/ore, then returns to the field or offers exit(s). A non-raid main MVP can also trigger the victory choice. Ordinary elites, non-MVP bosses and Hell shadows don't clear the map. `main.gd:4515–4517,5049–5061,11324–11330,11397–11404,11514–11567,11702–11717`; `mvp_raid.gd:110–157,218–238,425–452,1337–1411,1523–1531`
  - In a normal raid, boss HP is spawn-scaled HP × `6.75 × 1.3 × (1+mvp_hp) × boss_hp`, floored at `45 × boss_hp × (1.3 × party_dealt/max(30,measure_time()))`, then adjusted for the Tough oath and any gentle-map multiplier. Damage also uses the raid multiplier. [INFERENCE] A stronger field build can raise the raid's HP floor; an earlier portal is only a wall-time gain if the resulting fight still closes quickly. `mvp_raid.gd:455–479`; `game_data.gd:1623–1631`
  - Victory schedules `_pull_prizes` at 1.2 s and rechecks at 3 s, then retries every .5 s. The first 60 retries check `busy()` or lingering chest/card ground records; active `_loot` and queued party loot can delay it without that cap. XP, zeny loot and ore are also flagged `pull`, but are not in the ground-record wait condition. `main.gd:11547–11567,11695–11717,12939–12944`

## 4. Loot: from the ground to permanent storage

- Ground records live in `World.pickups` (`kind`, `node`, `pull`, etc.). `_update_pickups` handles hop, pet fetch if enabled, proximity magnetism and collection; XP/zeny/ore use `magnet*pickup_area`, other items `item_pull`. Chests collect at `<1.1`, others `<.6`; a chest can be held/blocked by busy UI. In party, eligible nearby standing heroes can pull shared pickups. XP and ores share to the group; zeny goes through per-hero share; cards go to the main save album at pickup, and equipment gets a copy during the run. Zeny credits the local save as earned in non-training runs, not just on victory. `main.gd:378–393,3918–3939,13816–13872,13898–13954,14041–14074,14153–14262`; `game_data.gd:9179`
- **Solo chest:** pickup makes the equipment rolls and runs `Equipment.receive` before the chest presentation. Auto-equip to empty slots and optional upgrade, bag, auto-sell/repeat-sell/fit logic are equipment policies, not movement AI. The presentation can still pause the game or get accumulated by `hold_chests`. With `menus.auto_pick`, the solo chest animation can still occur but the modal and first-time card reveal are skipped; card album and gear resolution remain active. Normal play may need to dismiss the chest/card reveal and release held levels/chests, but does not need to click every ground drop. Corrupt chests are a separate decision (see §8). `player.gd:4996–5004`; `equipment.gd:978–1021`; `main.gd:3921–3932,14176–14226,14298–14365,14941–14949`; `menus.gd:7110–7117`
- **Party chest:** per-hero ordinary gear is granted immediately; legendary/unique drops enter `loot_queue`/active `_loot`. `LootPanel` click desired items then `Listo`; one demander gets the item, multiple roll d100 (reroll top ties), unclaimed gear sells and shares zeny. Ready-all or elapsed 20 s resolves; unresolved choosers may hold countdown up to 45 s. `loot_auto` (`useful/all/pass` vs manual) skips unique decisions and does not confirm with uniques present. In contrast BotHero and local `menus.auto_pick` (except `--coop-manual`) preselect and mark ready: unseen by that hero's current worn/bag IDs and either unique or wearable; this does **not** guarantee the local hero wins. Gear/loot visuals remain; `loot_waits_for_me()` checks offer + live panel + not rolling + own slot not ready. `main.gd:3961–4009,4046–4158`; `loot_roll.gd:10–25,66–135`; `loot_panel.gd:60–77,81–138,188–202`
- **No auto-keep on the normal end screen.** `_end_run` clones transient, non-lent gear from worn slots and bag into `keep` (tags worn entries), sells transient bag gear and unslotted run cards, deposits found ores, and passes `keep_free = victory or run_ymir>0`. `show_end` sets `"end"`, shows `_keep_section` if nonempty and doesn't skip it for `auto_pick`. `_keep_offer` is the candidate list, not stored loot or necessarily all clickable entries. UI hides already-owned unique IDs, sorts by equipment score and starts on best available tier. Click an item to store it (three free after win/Ymir; otherwise one paid); if full, use the swap UI first. Stored copies lose run refines/cards. `_keep_pending` means the UI may warn about leaving unused selections, not a perfect storage eligibility check (full storage can still allow swap). Leaving through `_end_leave` cashes out unkept pieces, sells leftover worn value, and may require `Salir igual` confirmation. Restart via `Jugar de nuevo`, select destination, or go to town only after this choice. `main.gd:15683–15749`; `menus.gd:13529–13569,21372–21418,21452–21500,21543–21573,21581,21741–21813`; `save.gd:2070–2084`; `equipment.gd:958–962`
- `_keep_cost` = `max(fixed floor,ceil(Save.zeny()*share))`: normal rank 0–3 floors `0/300/700/1500`, shares `.5%/.5%/2%/4%`; legendary `4000` or `15%`, unique `4000` or `25%`. Buttons bind their quote when built. [INFERENCE] A budget based on run zeny alone will be wrong; use whole-bank price/available balance, and distinguish auto-selling from permanent keeping. `menus.gd:21416–21418,21475–21491,21807–21813`; `game_data.gd:5618–5625`

## 5. Party

- `coop_classes` is an explicit class list, not a random composition: startup `--coop` sets a Swordsman and `--coop=id,id` appends valid classes. `_add_party_hero` instantiates and sets up `Player`, assigns slot and `BotHero`, inherits XP/loot and legendary bonus parameters, spawns near the leader, connects level/death signals and creates per-hero flow data. Network host peers use the same construction, then replace input with `Remote` and use peer class/profile. `is_coop()` tests `heroes.size()>1`, including local bot parties. `main.gd:682–688,1409–1449,2114–2135,2188–2189`
- Standing NPC bots automatically refine, randomly choose a Homunculus when applicable, and at Lv20 choose `randi() % job_paths.size()`. `next_job(path=0)` itself is **deterministic**: clamps path, overlays reborn trans fields, chooses dance/female skill variants. `job_change` learns the default job starter; NPC pending levels use `Bot.pick_choice`. Local `menus.auto_pick` also picks a random job path unless its `--path` override is supplied; it skips the post-job starter dialog. Fight/support casting uses the same Player behavior for all heroes. `main.gd:4233–4251,14855–14873`; `player.gd:825–879,1775–1816`; `menus.gd:7926–7939`; `skills.gd:490–525`
- Per extra hero spawn rate `+60%`; enemy HP `+30% crowd/+60% tough/+110% MVP`. Each hero's shared XP multiplier is `(1−.15*(n−1))/(1+.6*(n−1))`, times `.5` for a party. [INFERENCE] More bots are not free farming DPS: encounter throughput, level curve and legendary roll competition all change. `game_data.gd:9048–9071`; `main.gd:4949,5473–5476,14168–14172,4046–4056`
- A fatal hit without a personal save emits `died`; party sets `downed=true` and resets `revive_t`. A standing ally within **3D** distance `<1.6` adds revive time, otherwise it drains at `1×`; threshold `3` s. Owning exactly one or more of `heal/sanctuary/gospel` is what makes a helper a healer for this check, not their class name or cast: up to `3×` progress and a tagged raise restores `50%` instead of `30%` HP. `_revive_hero` clears downed and supplies `2` s invulnerability. Party usually ends only on last standing hero falling, except a network local-player fall with no living non-`BotHero` member and no local autopilot. `main.gd:4208–4277,15644–15664`; `game_data.gd:2175–2176,9040–9043`

## 6. Altars and Hypnotist

- Normal altar timer is randomized `60–90` s, maximum five waiting, searches a spot `25–30` units from the hero with reachable/separation preference, and each lasts `120` s. Normal pool excludes NPC/karma altars, with Yggdrasil weight `.3` and default weight `1`. Nearest living hero inside flat radius `<1.8` channels by proximity for `2` s; outside, progress drains `1.5×`. Baldur also requires no hit for four **`world.clock`** seconds. While raid portal is open existing altars can still charge but don't spawn/age; other raid states suppress their tick. Tower limits rising to once per fight floor. `game_data.gd:8048–8104,8708–8710`; `altars.gd:29–39,90–173,178–244,272–275`

## 10. Endless: extend, farm, or finish

`continue_endless()` leaves raid, sets `endless=true`, records `_endless_start=time`, sets first event to `time+20`, warms `_endless_pick` and unpauses. `_endless_pick` samples all stage events except each stage MVP and any mob marked MVP (fallback: non-stage-MVP events on own stage). Once the original event list is exhausted, subsequent interval is 40 s; every fourth event is the **current stage** MVP, skipping the random event. Thus first repeat is nominally 140 additional **map** seconds, then 160 between MVPs, not counting fight/portal time. `endless_mvp_at()` uses the next event and modulo, `-1` when not eligible; guests get `endless_mvp_client`. A Valkyrie rush advances next MVP with a minimum five-second lead, rounds `_endless_count` up to the next multiple of four, and resets the schedule. ## Victory/defeat detection for an external driver

Use fields from the **current run**; `running` is also false in town/loading/after abandonment. `Player.fell` is set on actual `died` (including Doom) and is not reset by `_revive_hero`; `death_by={by,what,sprite}` may be populated on a death that personal revive prevents, so use it as diagnostics, not as a result flag. `world.boss` is often set for elites, can be replaced or nulled, and is null before a portal boss spawns. `Save.best(class).victory` is a historical OR, not a per-run result. `player.gd:242,3609–3614,3644–3671,3757–3781`; `main.gd:4268–4274,5275–5313,11514–11520,16009–16033,16068–16086`; `save.gd:360–375`
